Our Story

More than just
a coffee shop.

N.O.B (Nothing Ordinary) was born from a simple idea: to elevate the everyday. We blend specialty coffee, hearty comfort food, and a warm, inviting atmosphere across our growing family of outlets. Whether you're grabbing a quick bite on the go or settling in for a long chat, there's always a seat for you here.
